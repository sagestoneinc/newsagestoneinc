Shopify support gets easier when order issues, return questions, escalation paths, macros, and customer communication standards are documented clearly.

Where to focus first

Start with the places where customers wait, teams repeat the same explanation, or founders keep stepping in because the workflow has no clear owner.

What to document

Capture the trigger, owner, tool, customer message, internal note, exception path, and next step. A useful workflow is clear enough to repeat and light enough to maintain.

How this connects to customer experience

Cleaner operations make customers feel less friction. They also help teams improve what happens before support problems become urgent.
